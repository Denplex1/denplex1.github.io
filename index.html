I’m David Muiruri, the graphic designer behind Denplex Arts. I enjoy transforming ideas into clear and memorable visual communication. My work includes logo and brand identity, promotional graphics, social media artwork and print layouts.

I approach each project by considering its message, audience and visual direction. I value thoughtful composition, typography, colour and consistency, and I’m always learning and refining my creative process.
